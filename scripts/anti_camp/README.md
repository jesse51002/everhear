Rains TNT on any player who stays within four blocks of one spot for too long, until stopped.
Usage: anti_camp [seconds=15]
Ends: when stopped

- seconds: how long a player may stay put (at least 6). A warning shows 5 s before.

Seven primed TNT fall from 12 blocks up around the camper (tagged vfg_anti_camp), and the Demon Lord mocks them in chat. Explosions break blocks.
On stop: kills TNT still falling.
