Makes creepers turn invisible near players but gives them a three-second fuse and a loud hiss, until stopped.
Usage: sneaky_creepers
Ends: when stopped

No arguments. Creepers within 10 blocks of a player are invisible; within 4 they hiss loudly. Every creeper gets a 3 s fuse (normally 1.5 s) and the tag vfg_sneaky_creepers_fuse.
On stop: puts the fuse back to normal and removes the tag.
