Scatters chests of named guns and ammo 40 to 160 blocks around each player, marked with glowing tags, then ends.
Usage: gun_chests [player=@a] [per_player=4] [nearest=40] [farthest=160]
Ends: by itself

- player: round whom; per_player: chests round each, 1 to 10; nearest, farthest: how far from them.

Each chest holds two of: AK-47, Shotgun, Sniper Rifle, Rocket Launcher, Railgun (enchanted crossbows and bows) or a Harpoon Gun (trident), with ammo. Chests sit on the surface; above each floats a glowing "GUN CHEST" tag (an armor stand tagged vfg_gun_chest). Announces the drop in chat.
Leaves in the world: the chests and the tags. Remove the tags with vfg cmd 'kill @e[tag=vfg_gun_chest]'.
