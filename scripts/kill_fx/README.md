Plays kill-streak, hit-marker and hurt sounds to each player, until stopped.
Usage: kill_fx
Ends: when stopped

No arguments. Each player hears: a death sting when they die, a rising chime per kill (kills within 8 s make a streak; three or more add a twinkle), a hit marker when they deal damage, a thud when they take it. Uses the scoreboard objectives vfg_fx_death, vfg_fx_kill, vfg_fx_dealt and vfg_fx_taken.
On stop: removes the objectives.
