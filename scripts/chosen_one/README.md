Plays a private cinematic in which a radiant giant names a player the chosen one and gives them a holy sword, then ends.
Usage: chosen_one [player=@r] [blade=Holy Zombie Slayer]
Ends: by itself

- player: the chosen one (the first online of those named; @r one at random); nobody else sees or hears it.
- blade: the sword's name.

About 18 s: the player is slowed, a giant glowing Notch-skinned mannequin (tagged vfg_<instance>) appears, a title reads THE CHOSEN ONE, it whispers to the player by name, gives a netherite sword (smite 10, unbreakable), and vanishes.
Leaves in the world: the sword in the player's inventory.
