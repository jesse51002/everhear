Plays a private cinematic in which a radiant giant names the player the chosen one and gives them a holy sword, then ends.
Usage: chosen_one <player>
Ends: by itself

- player: the chosen one; nobody else sees or hears it.

About 18 s: the player is slowed, a giant glowing Notch-skinned mannequin (tagged vfg_chosen_one) appears, a title reads THE CHOSEN ONE, it whispers to the player by name, gives a netherite "Holy Zombie Slayer" (smite 10, unbreakable), and vanishes.
Leaves in the world: the sword in the player's inventory.
