Plays random Herobrine horror pranks on the named players, or everyone, every 60 to 150 seconds until stopped.
Usage: herobrine [player...]
Ends: when stopped

- player...: who gets haunted; none means everyone online. The clock only runs while a target is online.

Pranks: footsteps, a door creaking, cave sounds, a fake "Herobrine joined the game", a whisper in chat, a Herobrine statue behind the player for a few seconds (tagged vfg_herobrine), torches within 8 blocks removed, darkness and a heartbeat, a "behind you" sign, an enderman stare, distant lightning.
Leaves in the world: the signs, and the missing torches.
On stop: removes a statue if one is up.
