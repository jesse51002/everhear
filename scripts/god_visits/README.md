Every few minutes sends a giant god to a random player to give them a gift or take something, until stopped.
Usage: god_visits [min_s=180] [max_s=360]
Ends: when stopped

- min_s, max_s: the wait between visits, in seconds of someone being online.

A giant glowing god (The Radiant One, The Storm God or Thanatos; a mannequin tagged vfg_god_visits) appears in front of the player for 8 s and whispers to them alone. Six times in ten it gives a gift (diamonds, golden apples, a totem, netherite...), otherwise it clears up to 8 of one item (diamonds, food, arrows, torches...).
On stop: removes a god if one is visiting.
